The Arms of Government

There are three arms of Government which include;
Legislative: makes laws, declares war, levies taxes, approves money expenditures, & approves treaties.
Executive: enforces the laws, spends the money, makes treaties, & prints money.
Judicial: the courts - determines constitutionality of laws, finds innocence/guilt in criminal & civil cases.

Examples of the three arms of Government;

  1. Executive
    • The President/Vice President
    • Ministers
    • State Governor/Deputy Governor
    • LGA Chairmean/His Vice etc.
  2. Judicery
    • Jugdes
    • Magistrate
    • Lawyers etc.
  3. Legislators
    • National House of Assembly Members
    • State House of Assembly Members
    • Ward Councellors
